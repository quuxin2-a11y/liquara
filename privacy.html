Privacy

Liquara stores your files and everything it works out from them on your own device (in the browser or the Android app). If you have a paid license, Liquara also keeps a synced copy so your data follows you between the website and your phones. That copy is scrambled (AES-256) with a key made from your license key on your device before it leaves it, and is stored with our hosting provider (Cloudflare). You can turn sync off in Settings, and delete the synced copy any time in Settings → Erase all data or in My account.

Your account. My account uses your email with your license key, or a password you choose. If you set a password, it is scrambled on your device before it is sent, and we store only a salted hash of that, never the password itself. We keep your email, plan and license details so we can run your subscription. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your full card number.

When data leaves your device. Only when you choose to: (1) AI reading sends a file Liquara can't read on the device (for example a PDF, photo or unusual spreadsheet) to the AI service to be read, and the result comes back to your device; (2) the savings finder sends the name, category and amount of the bill you ask about; (3) live links you set up connect directly from your device to that system; (4) exchange rates are downloaded from a public rate service. Liquara does not keep copies of these on any server.

Payments. Card details are entered into Stripe's secure form and held by Stripe. Liquara never sees or stores card numbers.

Visitor stats. We may count website visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data.

Your control. Settings → Erase all data removes everything from the device and deletes the synced copy. Uninstalling the app does the same. You can set a PIN to protect Liquara on your device.

Terms

Liquara provides estimates and analysis to support your decisions. It is not a bank and does not provide financial, tax, investment or legal advice. Forecasts can be wrong; Liquara shows how accurate it has been on your own history so you can judge. Savings finder prices come from public sources and can change; confirm quotes before switching. Subscriptions start with a 14-day free trial and renew automatically until cancelled; you can cancel any time in the app.
