Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026

Liquara LLC ("Liquara", "we", "us") provides the Liquara website and apps. This policy explains what information we handle, why we handle it and the choices you have. In short: your financial data stays on your own devices unless you choose a feature that needs to send it, and we never sell your information.

1. Your financial data stays on your device

Liquara stores your files, and everything it works out from them, on your own device, in your browser or in the Android app. We do not receive this data unless you use sync (section 2) or one of the features described in section 3.

2. Sync between your devices

If you have a paid license, Liquara keeps a synced copy so that your data follows you between the website and your phones. Before that copy leaves your device, it is encrypted (AES-256) with a key made from your license key. The encrypted copy is stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. You can turn sync off in Settings, and you can delete the synced copy at any time in Settings → Erase all data or in My account.

3. When information leaves your device

Information leaves your device only when you choose a feature that needs it:

AI reading. If Liquara cannot read a file on your device (for example a PDF, a photo or an unusual spreadsheet), the file is sent to our AI service provider to be read, and the result comes back to your device.

Savings finder. The name, category and amount of the bill you ask about are sent so that prices can be checked.

Ask. When you type a question, a summary of your figures (totals, forecast, top customers and costs, not your raw files) is sent so that the question can be answered.

Sector news, competitor and lender searches. Your industry, region and business size are sent.

Live links. A connection you set up goes directly from your device to the system you connect.

Exchange rates. Rates are downloaded from a public rate service.

Liquara does not keep copies of this information on its servers.

4. Your account

My account works with your email address and your license key, or with a password you choose. If you set a password, it is scrambled on your device before it is sent, and we store only a salted hash of the result, never the password itself. We keep your email address, plan and license details so that we can run your subscription.

5. Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. You enter your card details in Stripe's secure form, and Stripe holds them. Liquara never sees or stores your full card number.

6. Website visits

We may count visits to our website with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and collects no personal data. Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them. As with any website, the companies that host our pages receive technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver them.

7. Emails and calls

If you email us, we use your message and your email address to reply and to help you. If you call us, we receive your phone number and any voicemail you leave, and we use them to return your call and to help you. Our phone service provider handles calls and voicemail on our behalf.

8. We do not sell your information

We do not sell your information. The companies named in this policy (Cloudflare, Stripe, our AI service provider and our website hosts) handle it only to provide their part of the service.

9. Your choices

Settings → Erase all data removes everything from your device and deletes the synced copy. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on that device. You can set a PIN to protect Liquara on your device. To ask for a copy of your account details, or to have your account deleted, email hello@getliquara.com.

10. Children

Liquara is a tool for businesses. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date, and we will email customers about important changes.
