Questions

About the app

Does the app cost extra?

No. Every plan includes the app. One license works on the website and your phones: up to 2 devices on Owner, 5 on Business and 25 on Enterprise.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. It installs directly from this page. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Will my data from the website show up?

Yes. With your license key entered, your company setup, files and settings sync between the website and the app. They're scrambled with your license key on your device before they're stored. You can turn sync off in Settings.

How do I update the app?

Download it again from this page. If your phone says the app can't be updated, uninstall the old one first. Your data comes back when you paste your license key.

Which phones does it work on?

Most Android phones from the last several years. On iPhone or a computer, use Liquara in your browser.
